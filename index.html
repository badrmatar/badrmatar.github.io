Research

  • VolPFN · MSc dissertation

    Jun 2026 – now

    A 4.75M-parameter transformer trained only on simulated market data (GARCH, stochastic, rough and regime-switching volatility with jumps). With its weights frozen, it forecasts full return distributions on real markets without retraining.

    Across 50,000+ market-days on 28 equity indices it beats GARCH-family, smooth-transition and gradient-boosted-tree baselines, with the gains holding after multiple-testing correction. It also transfers to stocks and currencies it never saw in training.

    PyTorchTransformersForecast evaluationPaper in preparation
